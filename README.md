## About

I'm a founder and product engineer based in Portugal.

I work across **product, software engineering and infrastructure**, turning ideas and ambiguous problems into production-ready systems.

My work usually sits somewhere between understanding what should be built, designing how it should work, engineering it properly and making sure the infrastructure behind it can support what comes next.

Today, most of that work happens through **Plain**, a Product Engineering Studio focused on helping companies decide, build and evolve software products.

---

## How I think about building software

> Good software starts before the first line of code.

I care about the decisions behind a product just as much as the implementation itself.

What problem are we solving?

What actually needs to exist?

What can stay simple?

What needs to scale?

And what will make this system easier — not harder — to evolve six months from now?

That intersection between **product thinking and engineering** is where I spend most of my time.
